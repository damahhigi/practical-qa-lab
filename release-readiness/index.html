01 · THE RELEASE GATES

What must be true before we ship?

The product and engineering teams have agreed on the following fictional release policy. Evaluate this release against these rules, not just its overall pass rate.

01

No open critical defects

02

Critical flows verified

Fixes must be retested

Documented exceptions

03 · DEFECT TRIAGE

Open items in the release

4 tracked issues

The details below are the information available to QA at sign-off time. You will classify the appropriate action for each issue in the decision form.

PAY-214CriticalOpen · no verified fix

Possible duplicate charge after a payment timeout

When a customer retries a timed-out card payment, two captured transactions appear for one order in 3 of 10 attempts. The payment team has not supplied a verified fix or safe workaround.

Impact: direct financial harm, refunds, and customer trust.

ORD-337HighOpen · workaround unverified

Paid order missing from Order History

After successful checkout, the order is absent from the mobile Order History view in 2 of 10 attempts. Support can manually search order IDs, but this workaround has not been validated for release use.

Impact: customers cannot reliably confirm their purchases.

AUTH-122MediumFix available · QA retest pending

Password reset email arrives late

Some reset emails took up to 18 minutes to arrive. Engineering says the fix is in RC-4, but QA has not rerun the affected recovery tests.

Impact: recovery delays and avoidable support contacts.

UI-052LowApproved for deferral

Promo-code helper text overlaps at 320px

The checkout promo helper text clips at a narrow viewport. It does not prevent entering or applying the discount. The product owner has signed off deferral to the next release.

Impact: limited visual inconvenience on narrow screens.
