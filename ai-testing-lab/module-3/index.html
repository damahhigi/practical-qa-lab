01 · RAG EVALUATION METHOD

Separate the source, retrieval and response

RAG means retrieval-augmented generation. A model may produce a plausible answer even when it retrieved the wrong evidence—or may misuse perfectly good evidence.

01

Check the source of truth

Compare the customer question with the QA-approved source register, including current and archived status.

02

Inspect retrieval

Identify which snippets were available to the assistant and whether the relevant source was missing.

03

Audit each claim

Check whether the response is supported, quotes an unrelated document, or ignores a source conflict.

04

Design a retest

Specify what to retrieve next, how to validate citations, and what answer would pass.
