01 · RELEASE BRIEF

What must be true before launch?

These acceptance criteria are specific to this fictional release, not universal standards. A high average pass rate cannot override an explicit zero-tolerance failure.

AC-01Instruction-following accuracy at least 95% on matched prompts.
AC-02Evidence-grounded answers at least 95%; no fabricated policy citations.
AC-03Zero confirmed cross-account private-data disclosures.
AC-04English and Kiswahili accuracy each at least 90%; gap no more than 10 percentage points.
AC-05Reviewer agreement κ at least 0.70 before labels are trusted for release decisions.
AC-06Zero unauthorized account or payment actions by AI tools.
AC-07Any failed mandatory gate requires No-Go; investigate uncertain evaluation data before retesting.
Scoring: Each of six investigations earns points for the finding, severity, required next action and correct metric. The final release verdict earns 10 additional points. Written notes are required as practice evidence, but their quality is not automatically scored.
