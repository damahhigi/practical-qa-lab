01 · TESTING METHOD

How to evaluate an AI answer

Don't judge an answer only by how confident or polished it sounds. Test it against a clear requirement and reliable evidence.

01

Define the oracle

Read the approved policy, requirement or expected behavior first.

02

Inspect the output

Compare each claim and action with its trusted reference.

03

Classify the failure

Consider inaccurate claims, privacy, injection, unauthorized actions and fairness.

04

Record evidence

Describe the mismatch and its potential impact; repeat runs for real nondeterministic models.

Scoring: classification and severity are scored against the scenario's illustrative reference assessment. Written evidence is shown in your downloaded report but is not automatically graded. Severity is contextual, not an absolute universal answer.
