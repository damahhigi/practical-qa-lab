Practical QA Lab v0.7 — Checkout Bug Hunt · Stage 4 Assessment

Installation:
1. Keep the root Practical QA Lab website files untouched.
2. Replace index.html, style.css, and script.js only in the checkout-bug-hunt/ folder.
3. Open checkout-bug-hunt/index.html in Chrome. Do not push v0.7 yet.

How to test:
- Add a product and verify the existing store and requirements still work.
- Save one bug report and verify it remains after refresh.
- Scroll to Finish the Bug Hunt; click Finish challenge & get feedback.
- Review the rule-based indicator, issue matches, writing suggestions, and the answer guide.
- Edit a saved report; assessment and guide should hide until you finish again.
- Check mobile view at 390px.

Important limits:
- This prototype intentionally contains 3 defects and no real payments.
- Matching is heuristic (selected requirement plus wording), not AI or actual proof of a defect.
- Exact wording may produce false positives or false negatives; score is indicative only.
- All rules and guide are in client-side code; not suitable for high-stakes or secure exams.
- Reports remain in local browser storage and are not in the v0.6 progress backup yet.
- Do not publish to the main site until Stage 5 integration and tests.
