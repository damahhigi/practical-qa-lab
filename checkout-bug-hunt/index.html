How this feedback works

This is a rule-based practice review, not AI grading. It looks for evidence of known defects and checks whether your reports contain useful reproduction details. A report that isn't recognized isn't necessarily wrong. The suggested score is an approximate practice indicator.

REVEALED AFTER FINISHING

Known defects and reproduction examples

These are the three seeded defects for this level. Your other observations may still be useful, even if they aren't among the known examples.

DEFECT A · REQ-02 / REQ-03

Cart subtotal can become stale after quantity changes

Try: Reset the shop, add a Notebook, then use the + button to increase quantity from 1 to 2.

Expected: Subtotal changes from KES 1,000 to KES 2,000.

Actual: The subtotal stays at KES 1,000 even though the cart shows 2 Notebooks.

DEFECT B · REQ-04 / REQ-06

Applied discount is not deducted from final total

Try: Add one Wireless Mouse and one Headphones (subtotal KES 4,000), then apply SAVE10.

Expected: Discount KES 400 and final total KES 3,600 with free shipping.

Actual: The discount appears and the code is accepted, but the final total remains KES 4,000.

DEFECT C · REQ-05

Shipping threshold excludes exactly KES 3,000

Try: Reset the shop and add two Wireless Mice (subtotal KES 3,000) without any discount.

Expected: Free shipping and final total KES 3,000.

Actual: Shipping is KES 250 and the final total is KES 3,250.
