01 · QUICK CONCEPTS

Assertions are your evidence

Playwright's await expect(...) assertions can automatically retry until the condition is met or the timeout is reached. Choose an assertion that proves the actual requirement—not just something that happens to be true.

01

Visible UI

toBeVisible() verifies that the expected element is visible to the user.

02

Exact feedback

toHaveText() checks the text of an element, such as a success status.

03

Correct destination

toHaveURL() validates a route or URL after navigation.

04

Application state

toBeChecked() proves a checkbox is selected—not merely enabled.
