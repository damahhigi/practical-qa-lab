01 · QUICK CONCEPTS

Find the cause, not just the symptom

A flaky test passes and fails without a meaningful product change. Diagnose why: a race, an unstable locator, a prematurely checked result, or shared test data.

01

Wait for a condition

Prefer an auto-retrying assertion like toBeEnabled() over fixed delays that can be too short or unnecessarily long.

02

Use stable locators

Identify an element by role and accessible name, scoped to its product card, instead of relying on DOM order or CSS classes.

03

Assert the final state

Wait for the expected async result with an auto-retrying assertion rather than checking an early snapshot.

04

Isolate test data

Generate unique signup identities to avoid collisions. Unique data helps, but does not replace cleanup or broader environment isolation.
